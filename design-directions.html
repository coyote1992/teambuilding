Poster

One ink on near-white paper. Slides are set as typographic posters: huge tight serif capitals, staggered line starts, tiny monospaced captions around the edge.

Type
Bodoni Moda 700 caps (text cut) for display, Bodoni Moda 400 for text, Spline Sans Mono for captions
Colour
paper #FBFBF8 ink #0C0C0C. No accent.
Best for
Cover, statement and closing slides, where one sentence should land from the back of the room.
Watch out
Long Hungarian words ("értékesítésig") limit how large the caps can go, and dense table slides need a calmer second layout.

Type

Agenda, principles, round steps and priorities: anything that is a sequence of named items.

Process principles

Matrix

The comparison table as the slide. Fine hairlines, tiny outlined tags for names, status marks for yes and no, and one tinted row to carry the takeaway.

Type
Albert Sans 500 for headings and names, 400 for cells
Colour
paper #F6F6F3 ink #141414 blue #1E3FD0 tint #E4EAFB
Best for
The four risk types, the pointless-versus-precise risk statement, anything that compares things across the same questions.
Watch out
The densest of the six. Cells have to stay at 20px or larger to be readable on a projector, which limits it to about five rows.

Risk statement

Timeline

A single vertical line down the middle of the slide, with stops that alternate left and right. Thin, large grotesk type, and your real flowchart used as the picture at each stop.

Type
Hanken Grotesk 200 for titles, 300 for stop headings, 400 for text (one of your two brand typefaces)
Colour
paper #F8F8F5 ink #0E0E0E. The flowchart supplies all the colour.
Best for
The process walk, the three rounds in order, and the final owners and deadlines.
Watch out
It fits three stops per slide at this size. Anything longer needs a second slide.

Drench

One saturated colour owns the whole slide, taken from the blue in the Fonte Viva wordmark. Each headline mixes a light small line and a very heavy large one, and everything else stays white.

Type
Familjen Grotesk 700 and 400, the brand display face
Colour
blue #1C33C4 deep #162AA5 white tint #C9D2FF
Best for
The three team rounds with their timers, and the real company examples. The room reads the colour as "this is the working part".
Watch out
A saturated slide is loud and costly to print, so printed handouts need a separate light version.

Planes

Content sits on stacked flat planes of three related greens, like cut paper. Each plane carries a circled numeral, a plain sentence-case title and one short line. The close goes tone-on-tone, green on deeper green.

Type
Archivo 400 and 500, sentence case throughout
Colour
stone #E7EBE3 leaf #BBD99A forest #1F3626 ink #14201A
Best for
Slides that show three or four parallel things: the three rounds, the three priorities, the closing.
Watch out
The planes need real content in each one. With one line of text they feel empty at this size.
