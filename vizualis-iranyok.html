Fonte Viva risk workshop: six visual directions

Each direction changes colour, type, spacing, layout and copy voice. They all show the same four slides, so you can compare them directly: the opening, one Collins lens, the process map, and a working round with the timer.

The content is a sample. The final copy gets rewritten in whichever voice you pick. Press Run timer demo to watch each timer count down quickly to zero and show its expired state.

02

A vonal felett Above the line

Built around Collins's own image, the "death line". Every slide has one horizontal line, and all content sits above it. During a round, a red bar fills that line as time runs out. The deck's structure carries the workshop's main idea.

Structure & spacing

  • A fixed horizon line at 76% of the height on every slide
  • Content above the line, metadata below it
  • The timer is the line: red fills it as time passes
  • Oversized type, few elements, tight leading

Copy voice

Direct and a little provocative. Asks hard questions.

„Mi sodorhatja a céget olyan helyzetbe, ahonnan nincs visszaút?”

Strong atIt has a clear concept and people will remember it. The design teaches Collins's idea without extra words. The timer feels part of the slide, not a widget stuck on top.

WatchThe "halálvonal" language is dramatic. It suits a leadership group but needs a light touch. Fonte Viva's brand colours play a small role here.

Mélyvíz Deep water

Dark and cinematic, with one centred focus per slide. A water metaphor runs through it: the risks are below the surface, and a faint waterline sits at the bottom of each slide. The timer is a large ring that works well as a focal point in a dimmed room.

  • Centred, symmetrical layout with one focal element
  • Lots of negative space, small edge metadata

Short and spare, like headlines.

Strong atIt has the most atmosphere and looks premium. The ring timer is the strongest focal point of the six, and the water metaphor fits a water brand.

WatchDark slides look washed out on weak projectors in bright rooms. Printing needs a separate light version. The map has to sit on a white plate.

„Kör 1 · P01.1 → P05. Mit kap a termelés, és mi van, ha rosszul?”

Strong atThe most useful for running the session. People always know where they are, both in the agenda and in the process. Screen and printout feel like one system. Each round gets its own colour.

WatchThe densest of the six and the most "tool-like". It needs firm discipline to avoid looking like a dashboard. Less emotional impact.

How to pick

Mixing is fine and often gives the best result. Some combinations that work:

  • 02 + 05: the death-line concept and its red-line timer, with the agenda rail and section colours for orientation.
  • 01 + 03: brand colours and arcs in a light mode, with the ring timer from Mélyvíz.
  • 04 + 06: editorial calm for the theory, then full-bleed colour blocks for the working rounds.
